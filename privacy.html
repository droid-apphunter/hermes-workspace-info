Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026. This policy applies to the privately operated droid Google Workspace integration used with Hermes Agent.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on the permissions granted and the requested task, the integration can access email and metadata, Drive files and documents, spreadsheets, calendar entries, and contacts. Read and write permissions are used to perform tasks requested by the account owner, such as preparing documents or organizing information. Authorization does not make Google account data public.

Processing and sharing

Task-relevant information may be processed on the operator's machine and sent to the configured AI model provider or other services necessary for a requested task. Those providers' own policies also apply. The operator does not sell Google user data or intentionally use it for advertising. The integration is not intended to use Google user data to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are stored separately from these public pages in a protected local credential store. Task outputs, local files, and conversation records may remain until the account owner deletes them. No credentials or private Google content are published on this website. The static hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The account owner can stop the integration, remove its access under Google Account third-party connections, and delete local credentials, task artifacts, and records. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete previously saved local outputs. There is no public user enrollment or public data-submission form. Privacy questions or deletion requests can be directed to the operator through the same private channel used to request tasks; no private contact address is published here.

Google API user data

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
